Shelfmates

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Shelfmates, operated by Jimin Seo, processes the information described below to provide reading records and social reading. For privacy questions, contact jasminmuze@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: username, email address, password hash, optional nickname and profile photo, and email-verification and password-reset records.
  • Reading information: EPUB files you upload; extracted book details such as title, author, ISBN, and cover; reading position, progress, and last-read time; and highlights, comments, and replies you create.
  • Social activity: likes, saved items, friend requests and relationships, and reading-room membership, invitations, and posts.
  • Notifications, support, and safety: notification settings and, if you enable push notifications, device push tokens; notification history; feedback and bug reports you submit; and safety-report details, including the reporting account, selected reason, and a reference to the reported account or content.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts; provide sign-in, email verification, and password recovery; sync your library and reading position; provide reading records, comments, and reading rooms; show social activity and deliver notifications you request; respond to support requests; review safety reports; and operate and secure Shelfmates. The app does not include features for ad targeting or selling personal information.

3. What other readers can see

Your username, optional nickname and profile photo, and reading activity you make public may be visible to other readers. New highlights and comments may be public by default, depending on your visibility setting. You can choose public, friends-only, private, or reading-room visibility for each post. Public reading rooms can be discovered and joined; private rooms are invitation-based. Your email address, password, and private original EPUB files are not displayed on public profiles. Book covers extracted from EPUBs may appear as shared book information.

4. Service providers

Replit provides hosting, database, and file storage. Resend, connected through Replit, sends email-verification and password-reset messages. If you enable device push notifications, your push token and notification payload are sent to Expo's push service. We share information with these providers as needed to operate the relevant features. Provider infrastructure may be located in a country different from yours.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain service information while your account is active. Safety reports are stored for review and are removed when the reporting account is deleted, or when the reported account or reported content is deleted with its owner's account. Email-verification codes are valid for 24 hours; password-reset codes are valid for one hour. You can change notification settings in the app or revoke notification permission through your device settings.

You can request permanent account deletion in Profile > Delete account or through the web account-deletion page. You must confirm your password. Account records and your authored comments and highlights, library, social relationships, notifications, feedback, and related reports are removed from the active database. Your EPUB and profile-photo files are queued for deletion with automatic retries. Files still referenced by another account may be retained. Other people's content is not deleted; replies they wrote and room history needed to preserve their contributions may remain. The account-deletion form also stores only the selected reason category, without an account identifier or other identifying fields. Operational logs and provider backups may follow separate retention cycles; contact us for details.

6. Your choices and contact

You can update your profile details, post visibility, and notification settings in the app. The app requests photo-library access when you choose a profile photo and notification permission if you enable device push notifications. For requests to access, correct, or delete your information, or for other privacy questions, email jasminmuze@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
